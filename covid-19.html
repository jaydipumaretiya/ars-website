    COVID-19 Compliance Services

    As the country adapts to the current times and prepares to reopen the workplace, our clients have told us they want assistance in assuring their customers and staff are in a safe place. Taking extra precautions is a big part of the foreseeable future in our efforts to reduce the transmission of the Coronavirus.

    Your COVID-19 Compliance Officer will work directly with you to maintain a safe and healthy, virus-free, workplace, eatery, hotel, gymnasium, or retail establishment. We know that this collaboration will lead to everyone being able to confidently return to stores, restaurants, and the workplace. Ultimately this means our clients can make a faster return to profitability.

    Our compliance procedures are firmly based on guidance that comes directly from CDC recommendations and the governor of your state. Our job is to make it easier for our clients to open their businesses safely and under the correct guidelines set out by state and government officials. We provide the security officers, we take care of all the compliance regulations, and our client gets to put their focus back on running their businesses.

    What will your COVID-19 Compliance Package include?

    There is a wide range of active measures involved with COVID-19 compliance, depending on your specific needs and requirements. The most common tasks we handle include:

    • Ensuring occupancy counts and social distancing spacing.
    • Implementing clearly marked queuing standards.
    • Monitoring body temperatures of employees, visitors, and customers.
    • Monitoring for proof of vaccination and/or negative COVID-19 test results.
    • Distribution of personal protection equipment.
    Which industries do we provide COVID-19 Compliance Services for?
    • Restaurants
    • Theaters
    • Retail stores
    • Hotels
    • Gymnasiums
    • Transportation
    • Special Event

    At all times, your COVID-19 compliance team will help you promote an atmosphere of safety and provide peace of mind.

    How does it work?

    We send our security teams to your place of business and consult on-site with your management team to create a plan for regulating and enforcing your organization's policies and COVID-19 measures. Going forward, the security team works in the background, implementing and enforcing the plan.

    Why choose Act Right Security?

    As the leading provider of security and safety services in New Hampshire, we develop and customize our services based on the specific needs of our clients - providing you with personalized, quality security services. Our COVID-19 compliance services can serve as a great example of how we adapt our security solutions to serve our clients, always responding with trained and dedicated teams of security professionals helping you create a safer environment.
