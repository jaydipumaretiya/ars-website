Careers

Reputable Employer

Act Right Security is among the top-leading, quality security providers of today. As we rapidly continue to grow, we are always looking for qualified personnel to join us on our journey. When you join our team, you'll join years of experience assuring peace of mind. Each and every one of our staff members are committed to serving, securing, and caring for the people and businesses we are relied on to protect in the communities we proudly serve. There are always amazing opportunities for you to build a career right in your very own community!

Diverse Workplace

We are committed to hiring and retaining a diverse workforce. Our goal is to increase diverse representation throughout our company. After all, we are Act Right Security!

Equal Opportunity Employer

All qualified applicants will receive consideration for employment without regard to race/ethnicity, color, national origin, ancestry, sex/gender, gender identity/expression, sexual orientation, marital/parental status, pregnancy/childbirth or related conditions, religion, creed, age, disability, genetic information, veteran status, or any other status protected by local, state or federal law. This policy applies with regards to all aspects of employment, including hiring, transfer, promotion, compensation, eligibility for benefits, and termination.

What are some basic requirements for employment with Act Right Security?
  • Must be at least 18 years of age to be employed as an Unarmed Security Officer.
  • Must be at least 21 years of age to be employed as an Armed Security Officer.
  • Must have a valid NJ driver's license and a safe motor vehicle record.
  • No criminal history (is preferred but NOT required).
  • High School Diploma or GED required an Associate's Degree in Criminal Justice preferred.
  • Must be a U.S. citizen or lawful permanent resident.
  • Fluent in speaking, reading, comprehending, and writing the English language. If you are also fluent in other languages, that's a plus!
  • Must be in good physical condition.
  • Previous law enforcement, military, or career security experience is preferred.
